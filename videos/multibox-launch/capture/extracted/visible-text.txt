MultiBox — by zer0ne. Free & open source. Windows 10 & 11.

Run the same Windows app many times, each with its own profile.
Separate logins, cookies and settings for every copy. Save the copies you want to keep, wipe the rest with one click.
No admin rights, drivers or virtual machine. Portable: one folder, no installer.

How to use:
1. Drop an app's .exe or shortcut onto MultiBox (or click Browse). It opens in a fresh, empty profile.
2. Click "Another copy". Each copy is a separate session you can sign in to with a different account.
3. Name & color each session (e.g. "Cline · Work", "Cline · Personal"). A colored border marks isolated windows.
4. Click "Save" and give it a name. MultiBox closes the app for a moment, copies its data, then reopens it. Find it in Vault → Saved.
5. Click "Wipe" to close a session and delete its temporary files. Saved profiles stay.

Works with: Electron · Chromium · Tauri · WebView2 · Node.js · Bun · Firefox · Microsoft Store desktop apps.
